## Three projects

### [Do LLMs Shape the Diffusion of New Software?](https://github.com/tubolyroli/thesis)
*MSc thesis · RDD + difference-in-discontinuities · 1st place, Corvinus TDK · OTDK nominee*

Do LLM training cutoffs create adoption barriers for new Python packages? Weekly panel of ~530,000 PyPI packages, placebo cohorts, clustered inference. The adoption gap emerges only after ChatGPT's launch — and a post-submission audit of the effect's robustness is documented in the repo, because stress-testing my own headline number taught me more than the clean result did.

### [Credit Risk as Profit Optimization](https://github.com/tubolyroli/lending-club-credit-risk) · [live demo](https://lending-club-credit-risk.streamlit.app)
*Calibrated LightGBM · SHAP · fairness audit · Streamlit*

Loan approval framed as profit optimization, not classification: **$64.1M realized profit vs $57.9M approve-all (+10.9%)** on a held-out 2016 cohort. Isotonic calibration, expected-profit threshold selection, a maturity-censoring bias diagnosis, and a disparate-impact audit.

### [Football Transfer Valuation](https://github.com/tubolyroli/football-transfer-valuation) · [live demo](https://football-transfer-valuation.streamlit.app)
*scikit-learn · Great Expectations · pytest + CI · Streamlit*

Leakage-safe pipeline predicting player market values: tuned Ridge at **€14.8M held-out MAE vs €17.6M baseline**, out-of-fold rankings, data-quality gates in CI. Extends my BSc thesis — a Blinder–Oaxaca decomposition of the Premier League transfer premium.
